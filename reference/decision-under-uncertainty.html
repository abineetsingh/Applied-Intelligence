Phase 6 quick reference

Decision Under Uncertainty

A compact set of calculations and guardrails for choosing when outcomes are unknown.

  1. List actions before outcomes; do not confuse what you control with what happens.
  2. Compute expected value as the probability-weighted average of outcomes.
  3. Represent nonlinear preferences, constraints, and catastrophic thresholds explicitly.
  4. Use decision trees for sequential choices, information arrival, and future options.
  5. Protect against ruin and irreversible downside before optimizing average payoff.
  6. Buy information only when it can change action enough to exceed its full cost.
  7. Prefer robust, reversible actions when estimates are fragile.
Guardrail

Expected value is a model output, not a command. Model error, dependence, utility, liquidity, and survival can dominate its arithmetic.
