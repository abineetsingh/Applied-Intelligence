First-Principles Operating System

A compact sequence for converting an ambiguous situation into a decision-ready model.

  1. Specify: name the decision, owner, deadline, objective, and scope.
  2. Atomize: list primitives, constraints, controllable variables, and unknowns.
  3. Separate: label observations, assumptions, inferences, forecasts, and preferences.
  4. Model: construct at least two plausible mechanisms or strategies.
  5. Quantify: estimate base rates, ranges, sensitivities, and thresholds.
  6. Target: seek the cheapest evidence capable of changing the action.
  7. Act: choose with a downside limit, falsifier, and review condition.
Guardrail

First principles do not mean ignoring accumulated knowledge. Use primitives to test whether an analogy fits; use base rates to avoid rebuilding reality from imagination.
