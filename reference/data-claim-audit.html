Data-Claim Audit

Questions to ask before allowing a chart, metric, or dataset to influence a decision.

  1. What process generated each observation, and what process is the claim about?
  2. What is the population, sampling frame, sample, parameter, and estimate?
  3. Which denominator, time window, and unit of analysis are being used?
  4. What does the full distribution show beyond the average?
  5. How large is ordinary variation, and are tails or path dependence decision-relevant?
  6. Which observations were selected, excluded, censored, or never measured?
  7. What range of values remains plausible, and would that range change the action?
Guardrail

A clean visualization cannot repair a contaminated data-generating process. Investigate provenance before precision.
