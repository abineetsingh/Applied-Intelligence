Honest Strategy Test

A pre-capital checklist for turning market patterns into falsifiable, cost-inclusive claims.

  1. Define instrument universe, horizon, signal, entry, exit, sizing, and benchmark before testing.
  2. Use information available at each historical timestamp; audit leakage and survivorship.
  3. Include spreads, fees, slippage, borrow, funding, taxes, and market impact where relevant.
  4. Report the complete return and drawdown distribution across regimes—not average return alone.
  5. Count every strategy, parameter, asset, feature, and sample tried.
  6. Reserve untouched chronological data and demand robustness around nearby parameters.
  7. Size for estimation error, correlation, losing sequences, and continued participation.
Guardrail

This material develops analytical discipline, not personalized financial advice or a promise that technical analysis produces profitable signals.
