Probability and Updating

A compact guide for translating uncertainty into numbers and revising them when evidence arrives.

  1. Define the event, deadline, and resolution rule before assigning a probability.
  2. Begin with a relevant base rate, then adjust for case-specific evidence.
  3. Use complements: P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
  4. Distinguish joint probability P(A and B) from conditional probability P(A given B).
  5. Translate percentages into natural frequencies when conditional reasoning feels slippery.
  6. Update according to likelihood: evidence matters when competing hypotheses predict it differently.
  7. Score resolved binary forecasts with Brier score: (forecast − outcome)².
Guardrail

A precise number can conceal a vague event or invented evidence. Precision in notation does not create precision in knowledge.
