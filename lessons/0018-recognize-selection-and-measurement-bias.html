Recognize selection and measurement bias

Study who is missing, why they are missing, and how labels were produced.

Mission connection

Data becomes leverage only when you understand how it was generated, how it varies, and what selection hid from view.

Retrieve before reading

What is the key limitation of simulation?

Reveal after answering

It reveals consequences of modeled assumptions but does not establish that the assumptions or structure are true.

The mental model

Selection bias changes which cases become visible. Survivorship removes failures; nonresponse removes people who decline; measurement bias shifts recorded values away from the intended construct. More data repeats these distortions more precisely.

  1. Define the cases that could have appeared but did not.
  2. Ask whether visibility depends on outcome or exposure.
  3. Separate the construct from its proxy measurement.
  4. Identify incentives affecting reports and labels.
  5. Seek negative cases and alternative measurements.

Worked case: learning from famous founders

Biographies select visible winners, reconstruct decisions after outcomes, and omit similar founders who behaved alike but failed. Their stories can generate hypotheses, but cannot estimate whether the behavior caused success without comparison cases.

Deliberate practice

Choose one dataset or set of anecdotes influencing you. Write the invisible population, the mechanism selecting visible cases, and one proxy that may not measure the intended construct.
