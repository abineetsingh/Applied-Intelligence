Read incentives and information flow

Predict organizational behavior from rewards, decision rights, and who learns what when.

Mission connection

Top-tier judgment recognizes when intuition is earned, finds leverage in systems, aligns incentives, and scales asymmetric bets without confusing visibility with causality.

Retrieve before reading

Why can improving engineering speed reduce rather than increase strategic learning?

Reveal after answering

If customer access is the constraint, faster development creates more unvalidated work without increasing evidence throughput.

The mental model

Organizations are information-processing systems populated by agents with different incentives. Metrics, ownership, career risk, delays, and escalation paths determine what leaders see and what employees rationally conceal or optimize.

  1. Map actors, objectives, incentives, and veto rights.
  2. Trace information from source to decision maker.
  3. Identify delay, filtering, and metric gaming.
  4. Separate responsibility from actual authority.
  5. Redesign feedback and rewards around system outcomes.

Worked case: a sales organization promises unsupported features

Sales is rewarded for bookings, product bears delivery cost, and leadership sees pipeline before churn. The behavior is not a communication accident; incentives and delayed feedback make overpromising locally rational.

Deliberate practice

Choose one recurring organizational failure. Create an actor-incentive-information map and redesign one reward, decision right, or feedback path.

Deliverable: An organizational mechanism map and one testable redesign.
