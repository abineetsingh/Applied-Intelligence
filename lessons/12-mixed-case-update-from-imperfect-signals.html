Mixed case: update from imperfect signals

Combine base rates, natural frequencies, dependence, and Bayesian updating.

Mission connection

Limited information never disappears. Exceptional judgment makes uncertainty explicit, comparable, updateable, and accountable.

Retrieve before reading

Complete the sentence: evidence is diagnostic when…

Reveal after answering

…it is substantially more likely under one hypothesis than under its competitors.

The mental model

Real signals arrive in clusters, share causes, and tempt double counting. Begin with the outside view, translate the strongest conditional claim into frequencies, then discount evidence that is redundant with evidence already counted.

  1. Define the forecast and relevant reference class.
  2. List signals and their plausible common causes.
  3. Convert the strongest signal into a frequency tree.
  4. Update once for correlated evidence, not once per observation.
  5. State the posterior as a range if inputs are fragile.

Worked case: choosing whether to build an analytics product

Four founders request the tool, keyword traffic is rising, and two competitors raised money. These are not three independent confirmations: attention from the same trend may drive all of them. Start with the base rate for tools sold to this buyer, then ask for costly behavior such as data access, implementation effort, or a paid design partnership.

Deliberate practice

Under a 15-minute timer, estimate whether this opportunity deserves a two-week validation sprint. Use a range, identify correlated signals, and specify the one observation with highest expected information value.
