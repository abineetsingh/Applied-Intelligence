Price the value of information

Research only when an answer can change action enough to justify cost and delay.

Mission connection

Insight becomes judgment only when it changes action while respecting values, downside, reversibility, and the cost of delay.

Retrieve before reading

Why is a favorable upside-to-downside ratio insufficient?

Reveal after answering

The downside may cause ruin, repeat frequently, be correlated across bets, or be underestimated; survival and path matter.

The mental model

Information has decision value when different possible findings lead to different actions and those improved actions outweigh research cost, delay, and implementation. Interesting information with no action consequence has little decision value.

  1. Write the current best action without new information.
  2. List possible research findings.
  3. Map each finding to a changed or unchanged action.
  4. Estimate improvement from switching actions.
  5. Subtract research cost and delay risk.

Worked case: another month of customer interviews

If every plausible interview outcome still leads to a cheap prototype, more interviews have low decision value. Run the prototype. If a specific buyer constraint would select between two expensive architectures, targeted research may be valuable.

Deliberate practice

For one research task on your list, create a result-to-action table. Cancel, narrow, or proceed based on whether findings can alter action.

Deliverable: A value-of-information table and research decision.

Check your judgment

Answer before rereading. Feedback appears immediately.

Transfer it

Do not add another trading indicator unless its possible values produce different entries, exits, sizes, or rejection decisions.
