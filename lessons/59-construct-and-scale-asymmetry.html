Construct and scale asymmetry

Build a portfolio where losses teach cheaply and winners earn increasing commitment.

Mission connection

Top-tier judgment recognizes when intuition is earned, finds leverage in systems, aligns incentives, and scales asymmetric bets without confusing visibility with causality.

Retrieve before reading

What three organizational elements often explain behavior better than stated intentions?

Reveal after answering

Incentives, information flows, and actual decision rights—along with delays, career risk, and metric design.

The mental model

Asymmetry is engineered through bounded experiments, preserved options, independent shots, and evidence-gated scaling. Reliability comes from portfolio process: many losses can coexist with superior outcomes if downside is capped and winners receive enough capital.

  1. Define maximum affordable loss per experiment.
  2. Diversify mechanisms, not labels.
  3. Set evidence gates before emotional commitment.
  4. Kill failed theses without starving live options.
  5. Scale winners while monitoring capacity and changed incentives.

Worked case: a founder choosing three markets

Instead of fully building for the favorite market, run three bounded tests tied to costly customer behavior. Allocate the next month based on evidence, not equal fairness. The portfolio is designed for cheap disconfirmation and concentrated follow-through.

Deliberate practice

Construct a portfolio of five current opportunities. For each, specify loss cap, independent thesis, evidence gate, kill rule, and scale rule.

Deliverable: An asymmetric opportunity portfolio and capital-allocation rule.

Transfer it

Do not call a concentrated position asymmetric merely because upside is large. Downside, dependence, liquidity, and survival must truly be bounded.
