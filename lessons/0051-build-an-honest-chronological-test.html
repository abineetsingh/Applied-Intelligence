Build an honest chronological test

Make the simulated decision know only what the real decision maker could know.

Mission connection

Market judgment demands falsifiable edges, honest chronology, realistic costs, survival-focused sizing, and resistance to backtest seduction.

Retrieve before reading

What two steps prevent hindsight from defining a technical pattern?

Reveal after answering

Fix numerical labeling rules before outcomes and determine the signal using only information available at the real-time decision point.

The mental model

Financial data are ordered and adaptive. Random shuffling can leak regimes and overlapping labels. An honest test preserves chronology, uses point-in-time universes and features, models execution delay, and reserves truly later data.

  1. Freeze the research question and development sample.
  2. Audit every feature timestamp and data revision.
  3. Use point-in-time membership and delisted assets.
  4. Model next-executable prices and costs.
  5. Evaluate once on untouched later periods.

Worked case: backtesting index constituents

Testing today's index members back through history removes firms that failed or were dropped. Using the day's close to generate and execute a signal at that same close may also be impossible. Both inflate apparent performance.
