Mixed case: is the effect real?

Integrate effect size, uncertainty, errors, power, and search flexibility.

Mission connection

Exceptional judgment distinguishes a noisy estimate from evidence strong enough to change a costly action.

Retrieve before reading

Why is the best result among twenty tests not equivalent to one preregistered test?

Reveal after answering

The search created many opportunities for noise to win; selection changes the result's false-positive probability and requires correction or new confirmation.

The mental model

Evidence strength is a property of the full design and search path. A small p-value cannot be interpreted without the estimand, sample, power, multiplicity, measurement, and practical threshold.

  1. Reconstruct the question asked before data.
  2. Estimate effect and uncertainty.
  3. Build the error-cost matrix.
  4. Count analysis flexibility and repeated looks.
  5. Choose act, confirm, redesign, or abandon.

Worked case: an AI feature lifts engagement

One of twelve engagement metrics rises 8% with p = .02 after the team stops the test on a strong day. Retention is unchanged and inference cost rises. The result is exploratory; confirm the chosen primary metric and net value on a fixed new sample.

Deliberate practice

Write a one-page evidence memo for the case. Include the strongest argument for shipping and the strongest argument that the result is selection noise.

Deliverable: An estimand, search audit, net-impact range, and next decision.

Check your judgment

Answer before rereading. Feedback appears immediately.

Transfer it

Use the same memo structure before allocating capital to any strategy discovered through extensive backtesting.
