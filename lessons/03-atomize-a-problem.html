Lesson 3 of 67 · 15–20 minutes

Atomize a problem

Reduce complexity into primitives you can observe, change, estimate, or constrain.

Mission connection

Exceptional judgment starts by representing the right problem. A clean representation prevents speed from becoming fast confusion.

Retrieve before reading

Turn “I should start a company” into a decision with an owner, deadline, and alternatives.

Reveal after answering

Example: “By August 15, I will choose whether to validate problem A, validate problem B, or keep searching, based on access to users and evidence of costly pain.”

The mental model

Atomic reasoning is not making a longer list. It is finding the smallest decision-relevant units: actors, resources, constraints, incentives, flows, states, actions, and uncertainties. Stop decomposing when each unit can be observed, estimated, changed, or treated as a hard constraint.

  1. Name the actors and what each controls.
  2. Trace the flows: money, attention, information, inventory, or risk.
  3. Separate hard constraints from negotiable preferences.
  4. Mark variables as controllable, observable, latent, or external.
  5. Stop when further detail would not alter the decision.

Worked case: customers abandoning onboarding

“Onboarding is bad” decomposes into acquisition source, expectation set before signup, first required action, time-to-value, permission friction, error rate, user motivation, and follow-up. This reveals several mechanisms; a redesign is only one possible intervention.

Deliberate practice

Choose a product or personal problem. Draw five columns: actors, flows, constraints, controllables, unknowns. Put at least three items in each, then circle only the items capable of changing your choice.

Deliverable: A one-page atomic map with decision-relevant elements circled.

Check your judgment

Answer before rereading. Feedback appears immediately.

Transfer it

For a chart pattern, atomize the claim into instrument, horizon, entry rule, exit rule, market regime, costs, and forecast. The pattern's name is not a primitive.
