Find bottlenecks and leverage

Improve the constraint governing system output before optimizing everything else.

Mission connection

Top-tier judgment recognizes when intuition is earned, finds leverage in systems, aligns incentives, and scales asymmetric bets without confusing visibility with causality.

Reveal after answering

The mental model

System performance is often limited by one binding constraint. Improvements elsewhere accumulate inventory, complexity, or local vanity metrics. Leverage comes from changing a constraint, feedback loop, complement, or rule that affects many downstream outcomes.

  1. Define system output and boundary.
  2. Map stocks, flows, queues, and dependencies.
  3. Locate where demand persistently exceeds capacity.
  4. Test whether relieving it moves total output.
  5. Expect the constraint to migrate afterward.

Worked case: a product team shipping faster

Engineering throughput doubles, but customer learning remains slow because recruiting qualified users takes six weeks. More code increases unvalidated inventory. The leverage point is continuous research access, not another development optimization.

Deliberate practice

Map one system you control. Identify the current constraint, evidence it binds, an intervention, and the next likely constraint if relieved.
