Compare return distributions

Evaluate volatility, drawdown, tails, and path—not annual return alone.

Mission connection

Market judgment demands falsifiable edges, honest chronology, realistic costs, survival-focused sizing, and resistance to backtest seduction.

Retrieve before reading

List five elements needed to turn a chart belief into an edge claim.

Reveal after answering

Signal, instrument universe, entry, exit, horizon, sizing, benchmark, cost model, and falsification threshold are valid elements.

The mental model

Returns compound multiplicatively. Volatility reduces geometric growth, drawdown affects survival, skew and tails concentrate outcomes, and correlation can make apparently separate trades one exposure. Performance must be a distribution through time.

  1. Compute arithmetic and geometric returns.
  2. Inspect volatility and maximum drawdown.
  3. Examine skew, tails, and worst sequences.
  4. Compare recovery time and capital requirements.
  5. Decompose exposure by common risk drivers.

Worked case: two strategies with equal average return

Strategy A earns steady small gains with rare large losses; Strategy B has frequent small losses and rare large gains. Equal mean return hides opposite skew, margin risk, behavioral difficulty, and performance under stress.

Deliberate practice

Create two ten-period return paths with the same arithmetic mean. Calculate compounded wealth and maximum drawdown, then compare which you could actually sustain.

Deliverable: Two paths, geometric outcomes, drawdowns, and a survivability judgment.
